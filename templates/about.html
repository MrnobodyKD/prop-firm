ABOUT MEGAFUNDED

Your Trading Skills. Our Capital. Your Opportunity.

At MegaFunded, we believe that talented traders should not be limited by the size of their personal trading accounts. Your trading potential should be measured by your discipline, strategy, consistency, and ability to manage risk — not simply by how much money you have in your bank account.

Who We Are

MegaFunded is a proprietary trading-focused platform designed for traders who want to take their trading journey to the next level.

Whether you are an experienced Forex trader or an emerging trader looking to prove your abilities, our programmes are designed to provide a structured pathway to demonstrate your skills, develop consistency, and qualify for funded trading opportunities.

We believe that trading success comes from a combination of knowledge, discipline, patience, and effective risk management.

What Makes MegaFunded Different?

Five principles that shape every programme, rule, and trader interaction on our platform.

1

Accessible Trading Challenges

We aim to make funded trading opportunities accessible through competitively priced evaluation programmes and different account options.

2

Opportunities to Trade Larger Accounts

Qualified traders can access larger simulated trading accounts, subject to the applicable programme requirements and trading rules.

Performance-Based Rewards

Eligible traders can earn rewards based on their trading performance, subject to programme terms, conditions, and payout requirements.

4

Transparent Trading Rules

5

Built for Ambitious Traders

From beginners developing their trading discipline to experienced traders looking for greater opportunities, MegaFunded is built around the ambition to improve and grow.

Our Mission

To make funded trading opportunities more accessible by creating an environment where traders can demonstrate their abilities, develop professionally, and be rewarded for disciplined performance.

Our Vision

To become a recognised global destination for aspiring and experienced traders, empowering a new generation of disciplined traders through technology, accessibility, and performance-driven opportunities.
