Trading Rules & Guidelines

Clear, transparent rules designed for your disciplined trading success

  • 1 Step: 10% target, single phase
  • 2 Step: Phase 1: 8% | Phase 2: 5%
  • 3 Step: 6% per phase (3 phases)
  • Pay As You Go: Phase 1: 5% | Phase 2: 8%
  • Pay After You Pass: 1% target in 7 days
  • Zero Time Limits: On 1/2/3 Step & Pay As You Go; minimum 3 trading days

Risk Management

  • Maximum Daily Loss: 3% on 1 Step, 3 Step & Pay After You Pass; 5% on 2 Step Standard & Pay As You Go; 3% on 2 Step Pro
  • Maximum Overall Loss: 6% trailing / 5% static on 1 Step; 10% / 5% on 2 Step; 5% on 3 Step; 10% on Pay As You Go; 6% trailing on Pay After You Pass

  • No Lot Size Restrictions: Trade within standard margin parameters

Trading Guidelines

  • Trading Styles: Discretionary, swing, intraday, and approved EAs welcome
  • Strategy Consistency: Do not switch strategy mid-phase (e.g. manual to EA)
  • Consistent Execution: Maintain disciplined volume across 3+ trading days
  • News Trading: Permitted while adhering to daily loss limits

Profit Splits & Compliance

  • Profit Split: 80% on 1 Step & Pay After You Pass; up to 95% on 2 Step; up to 90% on 3 Step; up to 80% on Pay As You Go
  • Monthly Payouts: Processed in 1–5 business days via bank transfer or crypto
  • Prohibited: Copy trading across multiple accounts, account/device sharing

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about trading challenges, rules, and payouts at Mega Funded.

What is Mega Funded?

Mega Funded is a proprietary trading firm that provides skilled traders worldwide with simulated capital from $6,000 to $200,000 (and scaling up to $1,000,000) to trade financial markets. Traders who pass our evaluation earn up to a 95% profit split on simulated profits.

What trading challenges do you offer?

We offer five challenge models:
• 1 Step: One-phase evaluation (10% profit target) with an 80% profit split — the fastest path to funding.
• 2 Step: Two-phase evaluation (8% Phase 1, 5% Phase 2) with up to a 95% profit split — our most popular.
• 3 Step: Three-phase evaluation (6% per phase) with up to a 90% profit split — the lowest price.
• Pay As You Go: Pay Phase 1 now, Phase 2 only if you pass, with up to an 80% profit split.
• Pay After You Pass: 1% target in 7 days — start for just $7.20 and pay the activation fee only if you pass.

How much capital can I trade?

Account sizes range from $6,000 to $200,000 across our challenge plans ($15,000 to $100,000 for Pay As You Go; $15,000 to $200,000 for Pay After You Pass). Successful traders can scale their accounts up to $1,000,000 through our scaling plan.

What is the profit split?

Funded traders receive an 80% profit split on 1 Step and Pay After You Pass, up to 95% on 2 Step, up to 90% on 3 Step, and up to 80% on Pay As You Go.

How and when do I get paid?

Payouts are processed monthly after each trading cycle. Once eligible, you can request your payout directly through your Client Section. Payouts are processed within 1 to 5 business days via bank transfer or cryptocurrency.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept international credit/debit cards (Visa, Mastercard) and major cryptocurrencies. All fees are denominated in USD.

Can I hold trades overnight and over weekends?

Yes. Mega Funded permits holding positions overnight and over weekends across all challenge types.

Are there any minimum or maximum trading day requirements?

There is a minimum requirement of 3 trading days to ensure consistency. There are zero maximum time limits on 1 Step, 2 Step, 3 Step and Pay As You Go — you can take as much time as you need to reach your profit target. Pay After You Pass has a 7-day time limit.

What trading styles or strategies are prohibited?

Prohibited practices include: copy trading across multiple accounts, account sharing or reselling, trading on the same device/IP across multiple accounts, high-frequency trading (HFT), latency arbitrage, tick scalping, and exploiting demo-server feed errors. You must also maintain consistency and not switch strategies mid-phase (e.g. manual to EA).

Can I use Expert Advisors (EAs) or automated trading?

Yes, algorithmic and EA trading is permitted, provided your strategy does not employ prohibited practices (like HFT or latency arbitrage) and maintains strategy consistency throughout the evaluation phase.

Do I need to verify my identity (KYC)?

Yes. Before receiving access to a simulated funded account or receiving any payout, you must successfully complete identity verification (KYC) by submitting valid government-issued photo ID and proof of address.

How do I contact Mega Funded support?

You can reach our dedicated support team by emailing support@megafunded.online, via Live Chat in your Client Section.
