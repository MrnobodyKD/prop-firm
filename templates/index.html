    Frequently Asked Questions

    Everything you need to know about trading challenges, rules, and payouts at Kisiki Capital.

    What is Kisiki Capital?

    Kisiki Capital is a proprietary trading firm based in Dar es Salaam, Tanzania, that provides skilled traders worldwide with simulated capital up to $100,000 (and scaling up to $1,000,000) to trade financial markets. Traders who pass our evaluation earn up to an 80% (and up to 90% with scaling) profit split on simulated profits.

    What trading challenges do you offer?

    We offer four distinct challenge models:
    • Kilimanjaro (Two-Step): Traditional 2-phase evaluation (8% Phase 1, 5% Phase 2) with an 80% profit split.
    • Everest (One-Step): Faster 1-phase evaluation (10% profit target) with an 80% profit split.
    • Instant Funding: No evaluation required; trade a simulated funded account immediately with a 70% profit split.
    • Pay Later: Start your evaluation for a flat $15 upfront and pay the remainder only after you pass.

    How much capital can I trade?

    Account sizes range from $7,500 to $100,000 across our evaluation plans ($7,500 to $50,000 for Instant Funding). Successful traders can scale their accounts up to $1,000,000 through our scaling plan.

    What is the profit split?

    Funded traders receive an 80% profit split on Kilimanjaro, Everest, and Pay Later accounts, and a 70% profit split on Instant accounts. Traders can scale their profit split up to 90% through consistent performance.

    How and when do I get paid?

    Payouts are processed monthly after each trading cycle. Once eligible, you can request your payout directly through your Client Section. Payouts are processed within 1 to 5 business days via bank transfer or cryptocurrency.

    What payment methods do you accept?

    We accept international credit/debit cards (Visa, Mastercard) and major cryptocurrencies. All fees are denominated in USD.

    Can I hold trades overnight and over weekends?

    Yes. Kisiki Capital permits holding positions overnight and over weekends across all challenge types.

    Are there any minimum or maximum trading day requirements?

    There is a minimum requirement of 5 trading days to ensure consistency. However, there are zero maximum time limits—you can take as much time as you need to reach your profit target.

    What trading styles or strategies are prohibited?

    Prohibited practices include: copy trading across multiple accounts, account sharing or reselling, trading on the same device/IP across multiple accounts, high-frequency trading (HFT), latency arbitrage, tick scalping, and exploiting demo-server feed errors. You must also maintain consistency and not switch strategies mid-phase (e.g. manual to EA).

    Can I use Expert Advisors (EAs) or automated trading?

    Yes, algorithmic and EA trading is permitted, provided your strategy does not employ prohibited practices (like HFT or latency arbitrage) and maintains strategy consistency throughout the evaluation phase.

    Do I need to verify my identity (KYC)?

    Yes. Before receiving access to a simulated funded account or receiving any payout, you must successfully complete identity verification (KYC) by submitting valid government-issued photo ID and proof of address.

    How do I contact Kisiki Capital support?

    You can reach our dedicated support team by emailing enquiries@kisikicapital.com, via Live Chat in your Client Section, or by visiting our office at Victoria noble center, 10th floor, Dar es Salaam, Tanzania.
