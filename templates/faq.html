You choose an account size and pay a one-time evaluation fee. You then trade a simulated account and must reach a profit target without breaching any of our risk rules. If you succeed, you move to a simulated funded account. If you breach a rule, the evaluation ends and you can try again by purchasing a new one.
Profit targets vary by challenge type. Each challenge page on your dashboard displays its exact profit target, loss limits, and payout percentage. Always review the specific challenge details before purchasing — the numbers are shown clearly for every plan.
There is no time limit on standard evaluations. You can take as long as you need to reach the target, provided you do not breach a drawdown rule. Minimum trading days apply — usually 5 days — to prevent traders from passing through a single reckless trade.

Risk Rules

The daily loss limit caps how much you can lose in a single trading day. The exact percentage is displayed on each challenge page in your dashboard. If your equity drops below this threshold at any point during the day, your account is closed. This rule ends more evaluations than any other — most traders who fail do so because they breach the daily limit, not because they miss the profit target.
Maximum drawdown is the total loss you are allowed to incur from your starting balance. The exact percentage is shown on each challenge page. This is separate from the daily loss limit. You can breach one without breaching the other, but both must be respected.
Drawdown type is specified per challenge on your dashboard. Some challenges use static drawdown (fixed at your starting balance) and others may use trailing drawdown (which rises with your equity). Always confirm which type applies to your specific plan before purchasing.
Yes. Mega Funded permits overnight and weekend holding on all accounts. There are no restrictions on when you can open or close positions, provided you remain within the loss limits.
There are no arbitrary lot size restrictions, but you must respect margin requirements and the risk rules. Trading excessively large positions is the fastest way to breach a daily loss limit.
