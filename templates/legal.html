Challenges evaluate trading performance on simulated accounts. A successful evaluation proceeds to a simulated funded account. Passing does not guarantee payouts; payouts depend on compliance and review.

Challenge fees are the listed one-time prices. Payments are accepted by manual bank transfer and cryptocurrency in USD. Fees are non-refundable once trading starts, except duplicate payments and platform-side failures.

Rules and withdrawals

Each plan has its stated targets, loss limits, minimum trading days, profit split, and first withdrawal period. Payouts are monthly after each trading cycle and, when approved, processed in 1–5 business days. No payout is guaranteed.
